Draw across the graph to shape the base levels (thin bars). The vocal-tension slope is added on top at the source (thick outline = actual excitation). Harmonics above 40% of the sample rate are muted (red).

Source excitation – vocal tension & breathiness

Vocal tension (source spectral slope)

Breathiness (aspiration noise at the glottis)

Source = voiced harmonics + aspiration noise, summed before the formant cascade. At 0% breathiness the noise path is fully muted and the voiced level is unchanged.

Formants F1–F5 (frequency, bandwidth, Q, peak gain, range)

Five second-order peaking biquads in cascade: H₁·H₂·H₃·H₄·H₅. Q = F / BW; bandwidth is primary.

Formant clustering (singer's-formant region)

Two neighbouring formants form a cluster when their distance is below tolerance + sensitivity × (BW₁+BW₂)/2. Enhancement adds an optional, capped resonator at the cluster centre.

Head rotation → pitch

Maximum range is ±72 semitones; the resulting pitch is limited to C2–G6.

Eyes + eyebrows → volume

Volume factor = (1 − eye influence × eye closure) × (1 − brow influence × brow lowering). Eye influence 100% + eyes fully closed = silence; 0% = eyes have no effect. The Volume slider stays the upper limit.
