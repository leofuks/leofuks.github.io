Calibration
How to calibrate

Enable Linear mode, set both trims to 0 dB, place a sound level meter (or meter app) at the ear/headphone, play the reference and move "Offset" until the meter reads the reference level.

Drag the orange dot (415 Hz) to change its level. Drag on the graph to play the test tone.

Source: The Physics and Psychophysics of Music, by Juan G. Roederer (Fig. 3.15), apud Egan, J. P. & Hake, H. W. (1950), Journal of the Acoustical Society of America.
