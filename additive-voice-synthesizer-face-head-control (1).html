Dashed orange: formants F1–F4. Drag a line horizontally to change its frequency; drag its dot vertically to change its strength (above 100% emphasises, below attenuates); use the mouse wheel over a line to change its bandwidth. Everything stays in sync with the formant sliders below.

Vowels & formant filters

Off – F1, F2 and volume are manual.

Face control drives only F1 (lip stretch), F2 (mouth opening) and master volume (eyes + eyebrows). On activation, hold a relaxed neutral face (mouth closed) for about 1.5 s: your current F1/F2/volume become the neutral point, and movements are measured relative to your own face size and pose. Manual edits made while active move the neutral point. Video is processed locally and never uploaded.

Off – pitch is manual.

Independent of face control: turn your head left/right (yaw only) to move the pitch down/up around the pitch you had when enabling. On activation keep your head straight for about 1.5 s to calibrate. Changing the pitch manually moves the centre. It moves the main voice, so keep "Sustain main voice" on to hear it. Sensitivity, smoothing and calibration here affect only this control. Video is processed locally and never uploaded.
