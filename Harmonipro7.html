  Controls: hold a key to sound a note · click/touch the on-screen keys (drag to glide) ·
  ↑/↓ octave · Space or Chords plays the "Up next" chord
  (held, or press-on/press-off when Toggle mode is ON). Keys of the "Up next" chord are marked in every octave with a blue dot:
  white keys turn light blue and black keys turn white; all other keys keep their normal colours.

  Master sound (8 or the ⏻ button): OFF silences everything immediately and blocks keys/chords; mic detection, harmonic field and sample recording keep working.

  Sample: press Record sample, allow the microphone and hold one steady note (vibrato allowed) for 4 s.
  The microphone is released automatically; the pitch is detected and the Recorded sample instrument is selected.
  Notes and chords are then played by transposing your recording; the middle part loops seamlessly while held. The recording is never mixed into the output live.

  Harmonic field (4 or button): switch ON, then play the notes of the field (keys, screen or mic; marked with a yellow square).
  Switch OFF: only those pitch classes sound, are stored and are used for chord analysis; other keys are dimmed.
  Switching ON again starts a new programming. Switching OFF with no notes, Esc or a page refresh restores the full chromatic scale.

  Alt (left) = play the relative of the current chord (C → Am, Bm → D) ·
  AltGr / right Alt = play the IV-degree chord, same major/minor quality (G → C, Am → Dm).
  Both ignore the acquired notes for that one transition (but respect the harmonic field); normal selection resumes afterwards.

  Ctrl+Shift+Space or the Acquisition button = start/stop note acquisition (latched; notes still sound while stopped) ·
  Shift or the on-screen Shift button (hold) = pause input: notes sound but neither keys nor the mic are stored ·
  While Shift is held, Chords repeats the current chord without advancing ·
  Ctrl (hold) or the on-screen Ctrl button (tap on/off) = Space/Chords repeat the current chord while new notes keep being acquired for the next one ·
  The dropdown in "Up next" shows that chord transposed for C, B♭, E♭ or F instruments ·
  Copy sequence copies the full chord history (oldest → newest) to the clipboard ·
  Reset positions clears the note memory and restores octave 3 without stopping sound or mic; pressing Chords before any new note replays the last chord ·
  Esc full session reset · 1 mic on/off · - prefer-minor.
