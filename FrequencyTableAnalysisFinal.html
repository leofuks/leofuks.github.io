Vocal Pitch Recorder

Average fundamental frequency of individual vocalizations of up to 4 s each (12-TET, adjustable A4 reference).

How to use

  1. Open this page from an https:// address in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone), not inside WhatsApp, Instagram or a file viewer.
  2. Tap Start acquisition and allow the microphone. Measurement #1 starts after a short countdown and lasts up to 4 s.
  3. Tap Stop measurement to end it early. The audio recorded so far is analyzed.
  4. Pause as long as you like, then tap Next measurement. Tap End acquisition when you are done.
  5. Tap ▶ Play on any result to hear a sawtooth tone at that frequency (not available while recording).
  6. Use − / + next to a note to move it down or up one octave, and ✕ to remove a result before saving.
  7. Export CSV saves the results. Open CSV loads a saved file so you can view and play it again.
  8. Set the A4 reference (415–460 Hz). Notes and deviations (−50 to +50 cents) update immediately.
  9. If the mic is blocked: Android → lock icon → Permissions → Microphone → Allow. iPhone → aA → Website Settings → Microphone → Allow. Also allow the browser in your phone's privacy settings, then reload.
