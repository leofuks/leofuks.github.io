Safety
  • Output starts at 10% and always fades in gradually. Increase it slowly.
  • The red STOP button silences everything immediately.
  • Avoid high volume and long sessions near your ears; take breaks. Square and sawtooth waves sound louder and harsher.
  • Speaker-to-microphone feedback can become loud or unstable. If the mic overloads, output is reduced automatically; if it continues, output stops.
  • Audio stops when the app goes to the background.
Accuracy & levels
  • Frequencies are shown and set with 0.01 Hz resolution. That is not acoustic accuracy: results depend on speaker, mic, room, browser processing and FFT bin width (see report, typically ≈5.9 Hz).
  • Levels are uncalibrated dBFS (decibels relative to digital full scale, 0 = maximum). "@f" is the FFT level at the output frequency; "rise" is the jump above the recent median. They are relative values, not SPL dB.
  • Phone speakers produce little output below ~200 Hz, so low resonances may be missed.
Requirements

Use Chrome or Samsung Internet over https:// (or localhost). Allow microphone access. If denied: lock icon → Permissions → Microphone → Allow, then reload.

Note—

Sing or hum a steady tone near the drum. A captured pitch is only a candidate driving frequency, not proof of resonance. Echo cancellation is enabled and pitch detection pauses while the speaker plays, so speaker output is not mistaken for your voice (not guaranteed on every device).
