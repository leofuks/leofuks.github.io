Keyboard (monophonic)

—

Click, tap or drag across keys (lower click = higher velocity), or use computer keys Z…/ and Q…]. Page Up/Page Down shift the computer keys by an octave. One note at a time; organ sound (audio starts on your first click or key press).

Recorder & file

Idle

Recorded phrases: timing is stored in ms at a 120 BPM reference (PPQ 480) and the tempo slider rescales it. Opened files keep their own tempo map.

Transposition

Transposition is computed from the original notes (non-cumulative). Only note pitches change; timing, durations, velocities and other events are preserved. Channel 10 (drums) is not transposed.
