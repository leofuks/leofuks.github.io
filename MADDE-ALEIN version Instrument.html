Normal mode: click or drag across the keys to play equally tempered notes. Portamento mode: F0 follows the horizontal position of the mouse or finger continuously, with no steps between notes. Formant Following moves each formant F1–F5 to the harmonic of F0 nearest its set value, and updates whenever F0 changes. Computer keys Z…/ and Q…] play several notes at once, and ← / → shift the octave. Space turns sound on and off.

Live spectrum (0–8 kHz)

Dashed lines show the formants as they are applied. When Formant Following is on, the small grey ticks at the top mark the set (target) values. Drag a line to change its frequency or drag its dot to change its strength. Scroll the mouse wheel over a line to change its bandwidth. Each formant moves independently and may cross the others.

Vowel space (F1 × F2)

Camera control

The camera is off.

Jaw opening controls F1, lip spreading controls F2 and turning your head changes pitch (up to ±6 octaves). Closing your eyes and lowering your eyebrows together lower the volume: open eyes with relaxed eyebrows give full volume, and fully closed eyes with fully lowered eyebrows give silence. On the selected hand, spreading the fingers raises F3 and straightening them raises F4. When the camera starts, hold a neutral face (eyes open, eyebrows relaxed) and a relaxed, half-open hand for 1.5 s. Video is processed locally in the browser.

At each interval (1 s by default), a short microphone window (100–200 ms) is analysed. If the sung pitch differs from the note being played, the app moves to the nearest equally tempered note so you can tune to it. Keep "Sustain main voice" on, and use headphones to avoid feedback. Analysis runs locally.

Tonic / scale for head rotation

Click Select tonic, then play the root (the lower note) and a third above it. A minor third gives a minor scale (A + C = A minor), and a major third gives a major scale (A + C♯ = A major). Press Esc to confirm.

Camera mapping and calibration

Face → F1 / F2 (independent)

F1 – jaw opening

F2 – lip spreading

Head rotation → pitch

Maximum range is ±72 semitones (6 octaves); the resulting pitch is limited to the keyboard range C2–G6.

Eyes + eyebrows → volume

Eye closure is the eyelid gap relative to eye width; brow lowering is the brow-to-eyelid distance relative to face height (both eyes and both brows averaged). Both are measured against the neutral face. Attenuation = weight × eye closure + (1 − weight) × brow lowering, so the two signals work together: full volume with eyes open and eyebrows relaxed, silence with eyes fully closed and eyebrows fully lowered. The manual Volume slider stays as the upper limit.

Left hand → F3 / F4

Raw: —

F3 – finger spread (abduction)

F4 – finger extension

Separating F3 and F4

Spread is the average angle between neighbouring fingers, measured in the palm plane. Extension is the average straightening of the finger joints. Both are joint angles, so moving, turning or tilting the hand, or changing its distance from the camera, does not affect them. Each value is measured relative to the calibrated reference hand.

Computer keyboard

Up to 8 notes can sound at once. Note keys are ignored while you type in number fields.
